# Vessel

A product page for a made-up ceramics studio, where the vase is real 3D geometry turned on a lathe in WebGL.

https://bxzex.github.io/vessel/

Each silhouette is a profile curve spun into about 44k triangles, and it's rebuilt whenever you change the height, the belly or the shape. The normals come from the slope of the profile, which is why the light breaks over the shoulder of the pot the way it should. The camera backs off to fit whatever shape you pick.

There are four shapes, five glazes and three finishes, and the price updates as you go. If WebGL isn't available the page says so, and everything else still works.
