Made to order · four weeks

Every pot on this page is real geometry, turned on a lathe in your browser from the profile you choose. Change the silhouette and the surface is rebuilt, vertex by vertex, and lit the way the glaze would be.

Thrown on the wheel, then turned on the lathe once it is leather hard.

A profile is only ever a line. Spin that line around an axis and you have a pot. That is exactly what happens here, except the axis is in a vertex shader and the line is the one you set with the sliders.

The silhouette you pick becomes a radius function. It is sampled up the height of the piece, swept through a full turn, and the normal at every point is taken from the slope of the profile, so the light breaks over the shoulder the way it would on the real thing.

Glazes behave differently under the same light. A matte surface scatters it, a gloss returns a hard highlight, and the rim catches more than the belly because it faces away from you. All three are in the shading, which is why changing the finish changes the shape you see.
